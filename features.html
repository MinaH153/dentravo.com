Discovery

Find Clinics You Can Trust

Search for dentists near you with real patient reviews, verified credentials, and transparent pricing. Rankings are never paid — trust is our moat.

Filter by insurance, specialty, distance, ratings, and availability. Every clinic profile shows what matters: real photos, staff bios, services offered, and patient experiences.

AI Assistant

Pico AI — Your Dental Guide

Ask Pico anything about dental health across 24 clinical domains — from anatomy and pathology to insurance billing and emergency care. Snap a photo for instant analysis. Use voice hands-free. Powered entirely by on-device Apple Foundation Models.

Pico provides symptom triage with urgency assessment, explains X-rays in plain English, helps you understand treatment plans, and gets smarter over time with a self-improvement engine. Free for everyone, no subscription required.

Insurance

Insurance Clarity, Instantly

Scan your insurance card and instantly see which clinics accept your plan. No more phone tag with insurance companies. No more surprise bills.

Dentravo+ subscribers get the Benefit Tracker — monitor your annual maximum and deductible in real time, see coverage percentages for preventive, basic, major, and ortho procedures, get cost estimates with your specific plan, and receive countdown alerts at 90, 60, 30, 14, and 7 days before your benefits reset.

Booking

Book Appointments Instantly

Request appointments directly through the app. Get confirmations, reminders, and automatic calendar integration. No phone calls needed.
