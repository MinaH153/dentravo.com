Chairside AI is moving from novelty to governance problem.

The real question is no longer whether AI can mark radiographs. The buyer question is whether a clinic can audit, explain, govern, and safely escalate what the software shows.

Signal take

Dentravo should rank AI tools by claims scope, data handling, explainability, audit trail, clinician responsibility, and escalation workflow before any demo economics.

What clinics should ask before a demo

  • What exact findings does the product claim to identify?
  • What evidence supports those claims, and where is the clearance scope documented?
  • Can a dentist explain why the system highlighted something?
  • What patient data is stored, where, and for how long?
  • How does the clinic document clinician judgment when AI disagrees?

Why this matters for Signal

Dental AI products can create real workflow value, but sloppy claims can destroy trust. Signal should treat AI as clinical governance infrastructure, not magic. A beautiful demo is not enough.
