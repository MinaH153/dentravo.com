A pressure-feedback brush wins the practical lane.

This is not a medical claim. It is a product verdict built from source trails, feature verification, and claims discipline.

Why it won

Powered toothbrushes have category-level evidence that is stronger than manual brushing for some oral-health outcomes. Within that category, Signal rewards pressure feedback because over-brushing is a common practical failure. The iO Series 6 also avoids the worst flagship-price trap: paying heavily for features that do not proportionally change daily brushing behavior.

Negative notes

  • Replacement heads remain an ongoing cost.
  • App features are useful but not required for better brushing.
  • No toothbrush should be treated as a substitute for dental care.

Claims safety

Signal does not claim this brush prevents disease, treats gum problems, or replaces a dentist. The safe claim is narrower: powered brushing has useful category evidence, and pressure feedback can support better brushing behavior for many users.
