Why it won

Signal rewards the iO Series 6 because powered brushing has a stronger category evidence base than manual brushing, and pressure feedback can reduce one of the most common user errors: brushing too hard. The model keeps the useful premium features without forcing buyers into the most expensive bundle.

Why others lost

  • Flagship models add cost before adding proportional oral-health value.
  • Budget brushes often lose on pressure feedback and long-term ownership support.
  • Generic brushes without replacement-head availability lose on durability and support.

Who should skip it

  • People who only want the lowest upfront price.
  • People who will not buy replacement heads on schedule.
  • People with pain, bleeding, recession, or active dental disease who need dentist guidance first.
