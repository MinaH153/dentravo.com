How Signal judges dental products.

Dentravo Signal exists to make dental product recommendations harder to buy and easier to audit. Revenue follows judgment. It does not create judgment.

Scoring weights

Clinical evidence25
Dental validation20
Usability15
Durability15
Value10
Claims safety10
Support5

Publication rules

  • Every published verdict needs at least three credible source records.
  • Every product page must include negative notes and who should skip it.
  • Every monetized page must disclose affiliate or lead-gen relationships.
  • AI summaries are source-linked analysis only. They are not fake reviews.
  • Health claims stay educational and must not promise diagnosis, treatment, cure, or guaranteed outcomes.

Revenue firewall

V1 allows affiliate links and qualified demo leads only. No sponsors. No dropshipping. No paid placement. The score and verdict must be locked before any monetized CTA appears.

Source freshness

Consumer product pages default to a 90 to 120 day refresh window. Clinic technology and AI pages refresh faster when pricing, regulatory scope, integrations, or data terms are likely to change.

What Signal refuses

  • Vendor-written rankings.
  • Commission-weighted winner lists.
  • Unlinked AI claims.
  • Anonymous fake reviews.
  • Before/after imagery that cannot be verified.

Trust promise

Payments cannot change a Signal score, winner badge, verdict, category order, or negative finding.

Launch categories

Electric toothbrushes, water flossers, whitening kits, interdental cleaners, toothpaste and mouthwash, intraoral cameras, intraoral scanners, dental AI software.

Public files

Signal pages are exposed through sitemap, RSS, llms.txt, and clean crawlable HTML for search engines and AI systems.
