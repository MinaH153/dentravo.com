Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 5, 2026  |  Last Updated: March 5, 2026

Dentravo LLC ("Dentravo," "we," "us," or "our") operates the Apex mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our App.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information: Name, email address, and phone number when you create an account (including via Apple Sign-In).

Health & Dental Information: Dental health data you voluntarily share through Pico AI conversations, photo uploads of dental conditions, and dental health journal entries. This information is processed primarily on your device.

Insurance Information: Insurance carrier, plan type, member ID, and group number that you provide for coverage matching. Insurance card images are processed on-device and are not stored on our servers.

Location Data: With your permission, we collect your location to find nearby dental clinics. Location data is used only for search and is not stored persistently.

Photos & Camera: Dental images you voluntarily capture or upload for AI analysis. Photos are analyzed on-device using Apple's Vision framework and are not transmitted to external servers unless you explicitly choose to share them.

Voice Data: When you use voice input with Pico AI, speech is transcribed on-device using Apple's Speech Recognition framework. Audio recordings are not stored or transmitted.

Usage Data: App interaction data (screens visited, features used, search queries) collected for improving the App experience. This data is anonymized and does not contain personally identifiable information.

Device Information: Device model, operating system version, and app version for diagnostics and compatibility.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide dental clinic discovery, insurance matching, and booking services
  • To power the Pico AI dental assistant (primarily on-device)
  • To personalize your experience based on your preferences and usage patterns
  • To send appointment reminders and important notifications (with your consent)
  • To improve App performance, fix bugs, and develop new features
  • To comply with legal obligations

3. On-Device AI Processing

Apex uses Apple Foundation Models to power Pico AI directly on your device. Approximately 95% of AI interactions are processed entirely on-device, meaning your conversations, dental photos, and health data never leave your phone for these interactions.

For complex queries (approximately 5% of interactions), data may be sent to Anthropic's Claude AI service for processing. Before any data is sent to a third-party AI service, you will be presented with a clear consent dialog explaining what data will be shared and with which provider. You can decline, and Pico will provide the best on-device response available.

4. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with:

  • Dental Clinics: Your contact information and booking details when you request an appointment
  • Supabase (Infrastructure): Our backend database provider, which processes data under strict security controls with encryption at rest and in transit
  • Anthropic (AI Processing): Limited conversation data for complex AI queries, only with your explicit consent
  • Stedi (Insurance Verification): Insurance eligibility data for real-time coverage checks via industry-standard EDI protocols
  • Law Enforcement: When required by law, court order, or legal process

We never share your health data for advertising purposes.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • All data encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest
  • Sensitive data (insurance, health information) stored in the iOS Keychain with hardware-backed encryption
  • Row-Level Security (RLS) on all database tables ensuring users can only access their own data
  • HIPAA-level security practices for all health-related data
  • Certificate pinning for sensitive API connections
  • Session timeouts and automatic token rotation
  • Comprehensive audit logging for data access

6. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. Conversation history with Pico AI is stored on-device and can be deleted at any time. Booking records are retained for 3 years for legal and dispute resolution purposes. You can request deletion of all your data at any time (see Section 8).

7. Children's Privacy

Apex is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected data from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Correction: Update or correct inaccurate personal information
  • Portability: Receive your data in a portable format
  • Opt-Out: Decline third-party AI processing, push notifications, or analytics

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@dentravo.com.

9. California Residents (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to know what personal information is collected and the right to opt out of the sale of personal information. We do not sell personal information.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes via the App or email. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
