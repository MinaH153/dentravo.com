Frequently Asked Questions

Is Pico AI really free?

Yes. Pico AI — including text, photo analysis, and voice input — is completely free for all users. It runs on-device using Apple's Foundation Models, so there are no server costs to pass on to you.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Absolutely. You can cancel through your Apple ID settings at any time. You'll keep access to premium features until the end of your billing period.

Do subscriptions affect clinic rankings?

Never. Dentravo+ subscriptions give you personal tools like benefit tracking and cost estimates. They have zero impact on how clinics are ranked or displayed. Trust and transparency are fundamental to everything we do.

What iOS version do I need?

Dentravo requires iOS 26 or later. Pico AI's on-device features require an iPhone with Apple Intelligence support.
