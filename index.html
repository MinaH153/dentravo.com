Security & Privacy

Your health data is sacred. We treat it that way.

We're not in the business of selling your data. We're in the business of protecting it.

HIPAA-Conscious. Encrypted. On-Device.

Every feature is designed with HIPAA principles in mind. Pico AI runs entirely on your iPhone — your questions and photos never leave your device. Your insurance data is encrypted in transit and at rest with AES-256.

We don't sell your data. We don't track you across apps. We don't use dark patterns to harvest your health information. Our revenue comes from subscriptions, not surveillance.
