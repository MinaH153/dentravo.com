Security & Privacy

Your health data is sacred. We treat it that way.

Dentravo is built with a privacy-first architecture. We don't sell your data, we don't track you across apps, and Pico AI runs entirely on your device.

HIPAA-Conscious Design

Every feature is designed with HIPAA principles in mind. Protected health information is handled with the highest standard of care and encryption.

End-to-End Encryption

Your personal data, insurance information, and health records are encrypted in transit and at rest. We use industry-standard TLS and AES-256.

On-Device AI Processing

Pico runs entirely on your iPhone using Apple Intelligence. Your dental questions and photos never leave your device. Zero cloud processing.

No Data Selling. Ever.

Your dental data is yours. We will never sell, share, or monetize your personal health information. Our business model is subscriptions, not surveillance.

SOC 2 Roadmap

We're pursuing SOC 2 Type II compliance to independently verify our security controls. Because trust should be auditable, not just promised.

No Tracking. No Cookies.

Even our website analytics (Plausible) are privacy-first and cookie-free. We practice what we preach at every level.

Rating Integrity

Our ratings can't be bought. Period.

Every other platform lets clinics pay for better placement. We refuse. Dentravo's ranking algorithm is based on verified patient reviews, clinical credentials, and responsiveness — never on advertising spend.

This isn't just a policy. It's the foundation of everything we build. The moment rankings become purchasable, patients can't trust anything they see. We'd rather have zero revenue than a single paid ranking.
