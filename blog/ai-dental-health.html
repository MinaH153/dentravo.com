How AI Is Changing the Way We Think About Dental Health

When most people think of AI in healthcare, they imagine robots performing surgery or algorithms reading MRI scans. But some of the most impactful applications of AI are far more personal: helping everyday people understand their own health.

Dental health is a perfect example. Most adults can't tell the difference between a cavity and a stain, don't understand their treatment plan, and have never had a conversation about dental health that wasn't rushed through in the last 30 seconds of a cleaning appointment.

The Dental Education Gap

There's a massive gap between what dentists know and what patients understand. This gap leads to poor outcomes: patients delay treatment because they don't understand the urgency, skip preventive care because they don't see the value, or agree to procedures they don't fully comprehend because they trust their provider implicitly.

The internet hasn't helped much. Searching dental symptoms online leads to a mix of outdated articles, SEO-optimized content from dental marketing firms, and forum posts that range from helpful to terrifying.

On-Device AI: A New Approach

Dentravo's AI assistant, Pico, takes a different approach. Built on Apple's Foundation Models, Pico runs entirely on your iPhone. Your questions, photos, and health data never leave your device.

This matters because dental health is deeply personal. People want to ask questions they might be embarrassed to ask their dentist. They want to understand what's happening in their mouth without judgment. And they want answers they can trust, not advertisements disguised as advice.

What Pico Can Do

Answer questions in plain language. "What's the difference between a crown and a veneer?" "Is it normal for my gums to bleed when I floss?" "How urgent is a cracked tooth?" Pico explains dental concepts the way a patient friend who happens to be a dentist would.

Analyze photos. Snap a photo of your teeth and Pico can identify potential concerns, explain what you're seeing, and suggest whether you should schedule a visit. It's not a diagnosis — it's education that helps you have a more informed conversation with your dentist.

Voice input. Ask questions hands-free while brushing or driving. Pico supports natural voice conversation for a more accessible experience.

Treatment plan translation. Share your treatment plan with Pico and get a plain-English breakdown of what each procedure involves, why it's recommended, and what alternatives might exist.

Privacy as a Feature

Most health AI tools send your data to cloud servers for processing. This creates privacy risks and requires trust in the company's data handling practices.

Pico is different by design. By running on-device using Apple's Foundation Models, your dental questions and photos are processed locally on your iPhone. There's no server to breach, no database to leak, no data to sell. Your dental health information stays exactly where it should: with you.

The best privacy policy is the one where your data never leaves your device in the first place.

AI as a Bridge, Not a Replacement

Pico isn't trying to replace your dentist. It's trying to make your relationship with your dentist better. When patients understand their dental health, they ask better questions, make more informed decisions, and follow through on treatment recommendations.

That's the real power of AI in dental health: not replacing human expertise, but making it more accessible, more understandable, and more useful for the people who need it most.
