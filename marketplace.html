Everything Your Own App Would Do

Every feature a clinic app needs. Zero development cost.

Building a custom app takes months and tens of thousands of dollars. Dentravo gives you all of it on day one.

Direct Patient Messaging

Send and receive messages with your patients in-app. Appointment reminders, follow-up instructions, treatment updates — all in one secure thread. No more phone tag.

Push Notifications

Send targeted notifications to your patients. Appointment reminders, benefit expiration alerts, seasonal promotions, new service announcements. Reach them instantly.

Digital Check-In

Patients check in through the app when they arrive. No clipboards. No waiting room paperwork. Their info is already on file. Streamline your front desk.

Appointment Management

Receive booking requests, confirm or reschedule, send automated reminders, sync with your calendar. Reduce no-shows with smart follow-ups.

Loyalty & Rewards

Create custom loyalty programs. Referral bonuses, visit milestones, seasonal promotions. Turn one-time patients into lifelong advocates without printing a single punch card.

Smart Recall & Outreach

Automated recall for overdue cleanings. Benefit expiration alerts. Re-engagement campaigns for inactive patients. The outreach runs itself.

Review Management

Respond to patient reviews, request feedback after visits, track sentiment trends. Build your reputation in real time with verified reviews only.

Practice Analytics

Real-time dashboards: patient flow, booking trends, review sentiment, revenue metrics, retention rates. Data-driven decisions without a data team.

Services & Cosmetics Menu

Showcase your full service menu with descriptions and pricing. Cosmetic procedures, specialty treatments, package deals. Patients browse before they book.

Dental Summaries & Diagnostics

Share treatment summaries, care instructions, and diagnostic notes directly with patients through the app. They can review anytime, ask Pico questions, and stay informed.

Staff Profiles & Bios

Let patients meet your team before they walk in. Dentist bios, credentials, specialties, photos. Build trust before the first appointment.

Your Brand, Their Phone

Your clinic's photos, logo, colors, and personality shine through your Dentravo profile. It looks and feels like your app — because for your patients, it is.
